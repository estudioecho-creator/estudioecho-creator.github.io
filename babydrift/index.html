Free or Premium

The free version includes all three sounds and a small banner ad. It requires validated Wi-Fi and pauses when the screen turns off or the app leaves the foreground.

Premium is a one-time purchase: no ads, offline listening, and playback with the screen locked. The localized price is shown by Google Play before checkout. No subscription.

Android release in preparation. Availability will be announced after Google Play approval.

Keep listening gentle

Keep the device away from the crib and use the lowest comfortable volume. BabyDrift is a sound tool, not a medical device. Volume percentages do not represent a measured sound pressure level.
