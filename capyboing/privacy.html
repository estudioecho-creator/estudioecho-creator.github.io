CAPYBOING Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

CAPYBOING is provided by Regis Furtado Barreto, under the Estudio Echo name. Contact: estudioecho@gmail.com.

Information processed

The game does not require an account. Progress, coins, selected cosmetics, purchase receipts and ad-free status are stored on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling the game can remove local progress and unused coins. Eligible non-consumable purchases can be restored through the Google Play account that bought them.

Google Play Billing processes optional purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details. The app uses product identifiers and purchase tokens to deliver purchases and prevent duplicate grants.

Google AdMob processes advertising identifiers and other device identifiers, IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app interactions, advertising events and diagnostic information. These data are used to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and improve service reliability. Processing depends on your region, choices and the services enabled.

Sharing and security

Google provides advertising, consent and purchase processing services and may process data with its advertising and service partners. These services transmit collected data using encryption in transit. Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not operate a game-account server or sell your personal information.

Your choices and retention

You can choose whether to watch rewarded ads. Where available, use Privacy choices in the main menu to review your advertising consent. You can also manage advertising settings on your Android device. Purchasing ad-free access removes in-game advertisements. Purchases and refunds are managed through Google Play.

Local game information remains until it is cleared from your device. Google determines retention for data processed by its services under its policies. For privacy requests, including questions about access or deletion, email estudioecho@gmail.com. We can assist with data under our control and direct you to Google for its services.

Audience

CAPYBOING is intended for teenagers aged 13 and older and adults. It is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address the request.

Changes and contact
